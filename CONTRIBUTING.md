# Contributing to RoadWatch

RoadWatch welcomes code, documentation, public-data imports and good-faith camera observations.

## Camera reports
Report only publicly observable infrastructure from places where observation is lawful. Do not trespass, interfere with equipment, access private systems, or interact with a phone unsafely while driving.

A useful report includes coordinates, observation time, camera category when known, direction/bearing when known, and evidence/source information.

Do not guess a manufacturer or camera purpose. `unknown` is preferable to an unsupported classification.

## Verification
Independent corroboration matters. Do not create multiple accounts or duplicate submissions to manufacture confidence. Evidence conflicts should be preserved and reviewed rather than deleted.

## Privacy
Do not submit unnecessary personally identifying information, license plates, faces, private-address information, or travel histories. Public evidence should be minimized to what is needed to document the infrastructure.

## Development
Changes should include tests where practical, avoid secrets in source control, and document changes to public schemas or verification behavior.
